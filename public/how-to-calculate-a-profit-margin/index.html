How to calculate a profit margin

Formula (margin on revenue): Margin% = (Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100, where Profit = Revenue − Cost.

Example in RWF

Revenue 800,000 RWF, cost 620,000 RWF → Profit = 180,000 RWF.

Margin% = (180,000 ÷ 800,000) × 100 = 22.5%.

Example in XOF (FCFA)

Revenue 4,000,000 XOF (FCFA), cost 3,400,000 XOF → Profit = 600,000.

Margin% = (600,000 ÷ 4,000,000) × 100 = 15%.

Use X is what % of Y on the calculator with X = profit and Y = revenue, or compute the profit first with subtraction.
