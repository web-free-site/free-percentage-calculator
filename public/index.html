Free Percentage Calculator

Seven client-side modes: percent of a total, share of a total, change, discount, increase, VAT extraction, and reverse percentage. Pick a mode, edit the fields, and see the formula beside your result—works with any amount, including RWF and FCFA examples.

Frequently asked questions

Is Free Percentage Calculator free to use?

Yes. All calculations run in your browser with no account and no paid API.

Which modes are included?

Percent of total, share of total, percent change, discount, increase, VAT extraction from gross, and reverse percentage.

Can I use Rwandan francs or FCFA?

Yes. Use plain numbers; example chips load RWF and XOF/XAF (FCFA) sample amounts.

Are tax rates official?

No. Rates such as 18% are editable examples, not legal advice for any country.

Is data sent to a server?

No. Math stays on your device.
